# Privacy Policy

Last updated: {{ site.time | date: "%Y-%m-%d" }}

DartTracker does not collect, store, or transmit personal data to any server.

- Data storage: Game history and preferences are stored locally on your device using iOS storage (UserDefaults). They never leave your device unless you back up your device.
- Analytics/Tracking: None. No third‑party analytics SDKs are used.
- Network: The app does not make network requests.
- Account: No account or login is required.
- Permissions: The app does not request access to contacts, camera, photos, location, microphone, Bluetooth, or other sensitive APIs.
